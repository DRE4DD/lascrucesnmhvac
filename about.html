Who We Are

About Las Cruces HVAC

A free contractor matching and cost estimation service for homeowners in Las Cruces and surrounding Southern New Mexico communities.

Our Mission

Helping Las Cruces Homeowners Navigate HVAC

HVAC work is expensive, confusing, and high-stakes — especially in a place like Las Cruces, where summer temperatures regularly exceed 100°F and a failing AC unit isn't just an inconvenience, it's a health hazard.

We built this site to do two simple things: help homeowners understand what their HVAC project might cost before they talk to anyone, and help them quickly connect with licensed local contractors — without having to search through directories or wade through shady lead-generation forms.

We focus on Las Cruces and Doña Ana County — we're not a national aggregator sending you out-of-town contractors.

Our cost estimator uses real industry formulas — 25 BTU/sq ft base sizing — not made-up numbers.

We don't fabricate reviews, fake contractor names, or invent testimonials. What you read here is real.

We make it clear when something is an estimate vs. a fact, and when you need a licensed pro to verify.

Our Approach

What Guides Us

Accuracy Over Hype

Our cost estimator uses real industry formulas. We provide ranges, not precise quotes, and we're clear about the difference. We'd rather give you a realistic ballpark than an inflated number.

Local First

We focus exclusively on Las Cruces and Doña Ana County. We're not sending your information to contractors in Albuquerque or El Paso. Local contractors know local conditions.

No Pressure

We never pressure homeowners to hire anyone. Get matched with a contractor, talk to them — and decide entirely on your own. No follow-up calls from us, no spam.

Honest Information

We don't fabricate reviews, invent testimonials, or make up contractor profiles. Our FAQ contains real HVAC information from industry sources — not marketing copy dressed as facts.
