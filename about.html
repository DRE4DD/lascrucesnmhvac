Our process is simple and transparent:

  1. Use the Calculator: Answer 4 quick questions about your home - square footage, insulation quality, climate conditions, and current system age.

Understanding Las Cruces HVAC Needs

Las Cruces presents unique HVAC challenges that generic calculators don't address:

  • Extreme Summer Heat: With temperatures regularly exceeding 95°F from May through September, cooling capacity is critical. Undersized systems will run constantly and still fail to keep homes comfortable.
  • High Cooling Hours: Las Cruces averages over 2,000 cooling hours annually - more than double many other regions. This makes efficiency ratings (SEER) especially important for energy savings.
  • Desert Dust: Our dusty environment requires more frequent filter changes and coil cleaning than humid climates.
  • Temperature Swings: Daily temperature variations of 30-40°F put stress on HVAC equipment, making proper maintenance essential.
