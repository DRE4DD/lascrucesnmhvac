Our Services

We provide two things at no cost: a free HVAC cost estimation tool and free contractor matching for homeowners in Las Cruces and surrounding Southern New Mexico communities.

Our Free Services

How We Help Homeowners

We do two things — and both are completely free to homeowners.

🔗

Free Contractor Matching

Fill out our short contact form with your location, the service you need, and basic home details. We'll identify licensed, insured local HVAC contractors in your area and pass your information along. The contractor reaches out to you directly to schedule a visit and provide a quote. There is no fee to you — ever.

🧮

Free Cost Estimation Tool

Not sure what size unit you need or what it might cost? Use our online HVAC calculator. Enter your square footage and a few details about your home and get an instant rough estimate — including the recommended unit size in tons and a ballpark installation cost range based on current market averages.

We are not an HVAC contractor. We are a referral and information service. We do not perform HVAC work, set contractor prices, or guarantee any contractor's work. Our role is to help you find a qualified local professional.
In Our Network

HVAC Services We Can Match You For

Our contractor network covers a wide range of residential HVAC services throughout Doña Ana County.

❄️

AC Installation

New central air conditioning system installation for homes that don't yet have AC, or full system replacements when the existing unit is beyond repair. Includes ductless mini-split installation as well.

🔧

AC Repair

Diagnosis and repair of cooling system problems — warm air blowing, system not turning on, refrigerant leaks, compressor issues, frozen coils, and more. Our contractors can work on all major brands.

🔥

Heating Installation

Furnace installation (gas or electric), heat pump systems, and full heating system replacements. Includes sizing consultation to ensure you get the right unit for your home.

🛠️

Heating Repair

Furnace not igniting, heat exchanger issues, blower motor problems, thermostat troubleshooting, and all other heating system diagnostics and repairs.

💨

Duct Cleaning

Professional ductwork cleaning to remove dust, debris, and allergens from your home's air distribution system. Improves air quality and can improve system efficiency.

📋

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Annual or bi-annual maintenance visits that include coil cleaning, refrigerant level checks, filter replacement, electrical connection inspection, and overall system performance evaluation.

🌬️

Indoor Air Quality

Installation of whole-home air purifiers, UV germicidal lights, humidifiers, and dehumidifiers to improve the air quality inside your home.

🌡️

Thermostat Replacement

Upgrade to a programmable or smart thermostat to improve comfort and reduce energy costs. Our contractors can install and configure most major brands.

🏠

New Construction HVAC

HVAC system design and installation for new home builds in Las Cruces and Doña Ana County. Includes load calculations, ductwork design, and full system installation.
