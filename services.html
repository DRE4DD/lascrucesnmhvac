HVAC Maintenance Las Cruces NM

Regular HVAC maintenance is crucial in Las Cruces' harsh desert climate. Preventive maintenance extends equipment life, improves efficiency, and prevents costly breakdowns during peak summer heat.

  • Seasonal tune-ups (spring and fall)
  • Air filter replacement
  • Coil cleaning and inspection
  • Refrigerant level checks
  • System performance testing
  • Energy efficiency optimization

Why Maintenance Matters: According to the U.S. Department of Energy, proper maintenance can improve HVAC efficiency by up to 30%. In Las Cruces' climate with 6+ months of heavy AC use, this translates to significant energy savings on your monthly bills.

Recommended Schedule: We recommend servicing your AC in spring (before summer heat) and your furnace in fall (before winter). This ensures optimal performance when you need it most.
